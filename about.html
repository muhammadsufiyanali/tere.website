Our story

From one shared ride to a city-wide community

Our founders were tired of sitting alone in Karachi traffic every morning. They started sharing rides with colleagues using a WhatsApp group — and the group grew to 2,000 people in three months.

Today Tere is a full platform trusted by hundreds of thousands of riders and drivers in 12 cities. We're a local team building for local needs: affordable, safe and friendly travel for everyone.

What drives us

Our values

Safety first

Verified profiles, live trip sharing and 24/7 support. Every decision starts with keeping our community safe.

Greener cities

Fewer cars means less traffic and cleaner air. Together our riders have saved over 4,000 tonnes of CO₂.

Community

We believe travel is better together. Many of our riders have turned their daily commute into lasting friendships.

Fair pricing

Transparent prices with no surge surprises. Drivers keep up to 90% of every fare.

Truly local

Built in Pakistan, for Pakistan — with support in Urdu, English, Sindhi and Punjabi.

Always improving

We ship updates every week based on what our riders and drivers tell us.
